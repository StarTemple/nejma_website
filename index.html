Bio

Nejma Nefertiti has carved her place as one of Brooklyn's most formidable voices in Hip Hop, a lyricist known for fierce delivery, intricate wordplay, layered storytelling, and vivid, cinematic imagery. She was one of 12 winners of The Saga Continues Contest, created by Sway and RZA of Wu-Tang Clan, and her delivery has been described as "grimy and captivating," with critics praising her razor-sharp bars and next-level command of the mic.

She's traded verses with French Hip Hop legend Akhenaton on a Nicholas Craven production, appeared as a feature for Beat Junkies' DJ Rhettmatic, recorded a full album with Harlem producer Big French, and continues to add to an extensive catalog of collaborative releases with Napoleon Da Legend. In 2024, she took home Best International Hip Hop Music Video at the Golden Sneakers International Hip Hop Film Festival in London for "Soul Ties."

Her LP, Method To My Madness M.T.M.M., includes "Goddess," featuring De La Soul's Pos, rap veteran Shortie No Mass, and DJ Evil Dee. It joins a body of work that spans albums, EPs, and singles, from The Valley of the Queens, to Letters From The Akashic, to the Jayne Wayne trilogy, and showcases her range as a writer, vocalist, and executive producer.

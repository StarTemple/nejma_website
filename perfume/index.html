The House

Small Batch. Hand-Blended. Nothing Synthetic.

House of Hatshepsut is a collection of six scents, each a portrait drawn from the ancient routes of frankincense and oud, where sacred smoke met skin, spice met stone, and time moved with the weight of resin. Every bottle is hand-blended in small batches by Nejma Nefertiti under Temple of NeJ — no synthetics, no shortcuts.

Each scent is built in two forms: an alcohol extract for brightness and projection, and a jojoba oil attar for a closer, longer-wearing presence on skin. Formerly sold through Etsy, the full collection now lives here — and can be ordered directly on this page.

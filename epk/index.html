Bio

Nejma Nefertiti is a Brooklyn Hip Hop lyricist known for fierce delivery, intricate wordplay, and vivid, cinematic storytelling. She was one of 12 winners of The Saga Continues Contest, created by Sway and RZA of Wu-Tang Clan, and won Best International Hip Hop Music Video at the 2024 Golden Sneakers International Hip Hop Film Festival in London for "Soul Ties."

She has traded verses with French Hip Hop legend Akhenaton, featured for Beat Junkies' DJ Rhettmatic, and released "Goddess" ft. Pos of De La Soul, Shortie No Mass, and DJ Evil Dee. Her catalog includes Letters From The Akashic, The Valley of the Queens, and the Jayne Wayne trilogy.

On stage, she has performed everywhere from BAM and the Kennedy Center to festivals in Belgrade and Brussels.
